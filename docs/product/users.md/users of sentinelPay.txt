# SentinelPay Users

----------------------------------------

User Role: Fraud Analyst

Who are they?

A fraud analyst works in a bank or FinTech company. Their job is to investigate transactions that look suspicious and decide whether they are genuine or fraudulent.

What are their responsibilities?

Monitor suspicious transactions
Review alerts generated by the fraud system
Investigate customer activity
Decide whether to approve, block, or escalate a transaction
Record investigation notes

What problems do they face?

They might face:

Hundreds of alerts every day
Limited time to investigate
Alerts without clear explanations
Information spread across multiple systems
Difficulty identifying patterns quickly

These are exactly the problems SentinelPay aims to solve.

How does SentinelPay help them?

Displays all transaction details in one place
Shows why a transaction received its risk score
Lists which fraud rules were triggered
Helps organize investigations
Reduces time spent understanding suspicious activity

What can they do inside SentinelPay?

Version 1 only.

Log in
View dashboard
View transactions
Open transaction details
View risk score
View triggered rules
Add investigation notes (optional for V1 if you want to keep scope small)
